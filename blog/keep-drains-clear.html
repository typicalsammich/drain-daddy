How to Keep Your Drains Clear

Simple habits that help prevent recurring clogs and keep kitchen and bathroom drains moving.

Simple habits that help prevent recurring clogs and keep kitchen and bathroom drains moving.

Mind what goes down the drain

Grease, coffee grounds, wipes and other debris are common contributors to clogs. Use strainers where practical and keep non-flushable items out of toilets.

Use simple preventative habits

Regularly clear visible hair and debris, pay attention to drains that are slowing down, and address recurring problems instead of repeatedly masking them.

When to call a plumber

If the issue is recurring, involves sewage, active leaking, loss of hot water, or you are unsure what is safe to attempt, a professional inspection can help narrow down the cause before more damage occurs.
